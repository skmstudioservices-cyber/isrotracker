ISRO missions to know

  • Gaganyaan — India's first crewed spaceflight programme; uncrewed test flights precede the crewed mission.
  • Chandrayaan-3 — made India the first country to soft-land near the lunar south pole (23 August 2023).
  • Aditya-L1 — India's first dedicated solar observatory, stationed at the L1 point.
  • NISAR — NASA–ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar satellite for Earth observation, launched on India's GSLV.
  • SpaDeX — in-space docking demonstration, key to future Indian space station plans.

ISRO's launch vehicles

PSLV — the reliable workhorse for small/medium satellites. GSLV Mk II/III — heavier payloads and flagship missions; the LVM3 launches Chandrayaan-class missions and will carry Gaganyaan. SSLV — a small-satellite quick-turnaround rocket. Rocket module / NGLV — the next-generation heavy-lift vehicle in development.
